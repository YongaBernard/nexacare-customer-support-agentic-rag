# NexaCare Services — Company Overview

## Company Name

NexaCare Services

## About NexaCare

NexaCare Services is a fictional customer-service organization used for the development and testing of the Customer Support Agentic RAG System.

The organization provides digital products and customer services through an online platform.

## Customer Support Channels

Customers can receive support through:

- AI customer support assistant
- Human customer support agents
- Email support
- Escalation management

## Support Areas

The customer support team handles:

- Product information
- Orders
- Order tracking
- Shipping
- Returns
- Refunds
- Account access
- Billing enquiries
- Complaints
- Service issues

## AI Assistant Responsibilities

The AI customer support assistant may:

- Answer questions using approved company knowledge
- Explain standard company policies
- Provide order and service guidance when authorized
- Identify customer intent
- Detect customer sentiment
- Detect urgency
- Retrieve relevant information from the knowledge base
- Escalate appropriate cases to human support

## AI Assistant Limitations

The AI assistant must not:

- Invent company policies
- Create unauthorized discounts
- Approve refunds outside its authority
- Make legal decisions
- Make financial decisions
- Override company policies
- Expose confidential information
- Provide information that is not supported by approved knowledge sources

## Human Support

Customers may request a human support agent at any time.

Cases involving complaints, disputes, safety concerns, sensitive information, or requests outside the AI assistant's authority should be considered for human escalation.
