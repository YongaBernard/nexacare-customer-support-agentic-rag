# NexaCare Services — Customer Account & Billing Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard procedures for customer accounts, billing enquiries, and payment-related support.

This document is an approved knowledge source for the Customer Support Agentic RAG System.

---

## 2. Account Support

Customers may contact support regarding:

- Account access
- Profile information
- Password or authentication issues
- Account settings
- Billing information
- Payment enquiries

The AI assistant may provide general guidance based on approved knowledge.

---

## 3. Account Security

Customers must not share passwords, authentication codes, or other security credentials with the AI assistant or support agents.

The AI assistant must never request or expose a customer's password or authentication credentials.

Security-sensitive account issues should be escalated through the appropriate support process.

---

## 4. Billing Enquiries

Customers may contact support about:

- Unexpected charges
- Payment failures
- Duplicate charges
- Billing dates
- Payment methods
- Invoice questions

The AI assistant may explain general billing procedures but must not invent transaction details.

---

## 5. Payment Failures

If a payment fails, the customer may be advised to:

- Verify the payment method.
- Confirm that the payment information is correct.
- Check whether the payment provider has declined the transaction.
- Try an approved alternative payment method where available.
- Contact support if the issue continues.

The AI assistant must not claim to know why a payment failed unless the authorized system provides that information.

---

## 6. Duplicate Charges

Customers who believe they have been charged more than once should contact customer support.

The support team may review the relevant transaction information.

The AI assistant must not independently confirm that a duplicate charge occurred without access to an authorized transaction system.

---

## 7. Billing Disputes

Billing disputes should be reviewed by an authorized human support representative.

The AI assistant may:

- Explain the standard dispute process.
- Collect relevant information.
- Direct the customer to the appropriate support channel.
- Escalate the case.

The AI assistant must not make final decisions regarding disputed transactions.

---

## 8. Refunds Related to Billing

Refund requests must follow the approved Returns & Refund Policy.

The AI assistant must not independently approve or guarantee a refund.

---

## 9. Sensitive Financial Information

The AI assistant must minimize the collection and exposure of sensitive financial information.

Customers should not provide:

- Full payment-card numbers
- Security codes
- Passwords
- Authentication codes
- Private account credentials

Sensitive information must not be unnecessarily stored in application logs.

---

## 10. AI Assistant Authority

The AI assistant may:

- Explain general billing procedures.
- Explain approved account-support processes.
- Guide customers to appropriate support channels.
- Identify when human assistance is required.

The AI assistant must not:

- Change account ownership.
- Approve financial adjustments.
- Approve refunds.
- Confirm transaction details without authorized data access.
- Request passwords or authentication codes.
- Make financial decisions outside its approved authority.

---

## 11. Human Escalation

Cases should be considered for human escalation when:

- A customer disputes a transaction.
- A customer reports suspected unauthorized activity.
- A payment issue cannot be resolved through standard guidance.
- The customer requests a financial adjustment.
- Sensitive account information is involved.
- The customer requests a human agent.
