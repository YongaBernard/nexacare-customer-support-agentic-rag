# NexaCare Services — Returns & Refund Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which customers may return eligible products and request refunds.

This document is an approved knowledge source for the Customer Support Agentic RAG System.

---

## 2. Return Eligibility

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of receiving an eligible product.

The product must generally:

- Be unused
- Be in its original condition
- Include original packaging where applicable
- Include accessories originally supplied with the product
- Not show signs of intentional damage or misuse

Some products or services may have different return conditions.

---

## 3. Non-Returnable Items

The following categories may not be eligible for return:

- Digital products that have already been accessed or downloaded
- Personalized products
- Products damaged through customer misuse
- Products returned outside the applicable return period
- Items excluded from return eligibility by their product-specific terms

Customers should contact support if they are unsure whether an item is eligible.

---

## 4. Return Request

Customers requesting a return should provide:

- Order number
- Product name
- Reason for the return
- Date the product was received

The support team may request additional information when required.

---

## 5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are normally processed after the returned item has been received and inspected.

The refund will generally be issued to the original payment method.

Processing time may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

NexaCare support agents must not promise an exact refund date unless the date has been confirmed through an authorized system.

---

## 6. Damaged or Defective Products

Customers who receive damaged or defective products should contact customer support as soon as possible.

The customer may be asked to provide:

- Order number
- Description of the issue
- Photographs or other evidence where appropriate

The case may be escalated to a human support agent for further assessment.

---

## 7. Incorrect Items

If a customer receives an incorrect item, the customer should contact support with the order number and details of the item received.

The case should be reviewed by customer support before a replacement or refund is confirmed.

---

## 8. Refund Authority

The AI assistant must not independently approve refunds.

The AI assistant may:

- Explain the refund policy
- Explain return eligibility requirements
- Collect relevant information
- Guide the customer through the standard process
- Escalate the request to an authorized human agent

Refund approval must be performed through an authorized customer-service process.

---

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require review by an authorized human support representative.

The AI assistant must not create or promise policy exceptions.

---

## 10. Customer Complaints

Customers who disagree with a return or refund decision may request human review.

Complaints involving disputes, significant financial impact, or repeated unresolved issues should be escalated.

---

## 11. AI Governance Requirements

The AI assistant must:

- Use this approved policy when answering return and refund questions.
- Avoid inventing refund conditions.
- Avoid guaranteeing refund approval.
- Avoid promising processing times that are not confirmed.
- Escalate cases outside its authority.
- Identify uncertainty when the available knowledge is insufficient.
