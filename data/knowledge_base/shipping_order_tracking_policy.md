# NexaCare Services — Shipping & Order Tracking Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard procedures for shipping, delivery, and order tracking.

This document is an approved knowledge source for the Customer Support Agentic RAG System.

---

## 2. Order Processing

Orders are normally processed after payment has been successfully confirmed.

Processing time may vary depending on:

- Product availability
- Order volume
- Payment verification
- Customer location
- Operational conditions

The AI assistant must not guarantee a specific processing time unless an authorized system provides a confirmed estimate.

---

## 3. Shipping

Available shipping options may vary depending on the customer's location and the product ordered.

Customers should refer to their order confirmation for the applicable shipping information.

Shipping estimates are not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed by an authorized system.

---

## 4. Order Tracking

Customers can use their order reference or tracking information to check the status of an eligible shipment.

Typical order statuses include:

- Order received
- Processing
- Preparing for shipment
- Shipped
- In transit
- Out for delivery
- Delivered
- Delivery exception

The AI assistant may explain the meaning of an order status.

---

## 5. Delayed Orders

If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery window, the customer should contact support.

The support team may review:

- Order number
- Shipping address
- Tracking information
- Expected delivery date
- Current shipment status

Cases with significant delays may be escalated to a human support agent.

---

## 6. Lost Shipments

If tracking information indicates that a shipment may be lost, the case should be reviewed by customer support.

The AI assistant must not independently declare an order lost or guarantee a replacement or refund.

---

## 7. Incorrect Delivery Address

Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they identify an incorrect delivery address.

Address changes may not be possible after an order has entered the shipping process.

The AI assistant must not guarantee that an address change will be successful.

---

## 8. Delivery Problems

Customers should contact support if:

- An order is marked delivered but was not received.
- The package appears damaged.
- The wrong product was delivered.
- The shipment has stopped updating.
- The delivery is significantly delayed.

These cases may require human investigation.

---

## 9. Delivery Estimates

Delivery estimates are provided for guidance and may be affected by:

- Carrier delays
- Weather
- Operational disruptions
- Incorrect address information
- Customs or regulatory processes
- Unexpected demand

The AI assistant must not present an estimated delivery date as a guaranteed delivery date.

---

## 10. AI Assistant Responsibilities

The AI assistant may:

- Explain shipping policies.
- Explain order-status terminology.
- Guide customers through the tracking process.
- Collect information required for support.
- Identify potentially delayed or problematic orders.
- Escalate cases requiring human investigation.

The AI assistant must not:

- Invent tracking information.
- Claim an order has shipped without evidence.
- Guarantee delivery dates.
- Declare an order lost without authorized confirmation.
- Promise compensation or refunds without authorization.

---

## 11. Human Escalation

Cases should be considered for human escalation when:

- The customer reports a lost shipment.
- The customer reports a damaged delivery.
- An order is marked delivered but was not received.
- The customer disputes delivery information.
- The customer requests compensation.
- The issue cannot be resolved using approved knowledge.
