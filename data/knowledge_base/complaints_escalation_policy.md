# NexaCare Services — Complaints & Escalation Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how customer complaints, sensitive issues, and cases requiring human intervention should be handled.

This document is an approved knowledge source for the Customer Support Agentic RAG System.

---

## 2. Customer Complaints

Customers have the right to raise concerns about:

- Products or services
- Orders
- Shipping
- Refunds
- Billing
- Customer-service interactions
- Account issues

The AI assistant should acknowledge the concern and provide appropriate guidance based on approved knowledge.

---

## 3. Complaint Handling

The AI assistant may:

- Understand the customer's concern.
- Provide relevant policy information.
- Collect basic information needed for support.
- Explain the standard escalation process.
- Direct the customer to human support.

The AI assistant must not dismiss, minimize, or intentionally frustrate a legitimate complaint.

---

## 4. Mandatory Human Escalation

A case should be escalated to a human support representative when:

- The customer explicitly requests a human.
- The customer raises a formal complaint.
- The customer reports suspected fraud or unauthorized activity.
- The customer reports a safety concern.
- The customer disputes a financial transaction.
- The customer reports a significant service failure.
- The issue involves sensitive personal information.
- The AI cannot confidently answer using approved knowledge.
- The requested action exceeds the AI assistant's authority.

---

## 5. High-Urgency Cases

High-urgency cases should receive priority attention.

Examples include:

- Safety-related concerns
- Suspected account compromise
- Suspected fraud
- Significant financial disputes
- Repeated unresolved service failures
- Threats of immediate harm

The AI assistant must not attempt to resolve high-risk cases beyond its approved authority.

---

## 6. Customer Sentiment

The system may classify customer sentiment to help determine appropriate routing.

Possible classifications include:

- Positive
- Neutral
- Frustrated
- Angry
- Highly distressed

Sentiment classification should support human oversight rather than replace human judgment.

---

## 7. Escalation Information

Where appropriate, the AI assistant may collect:

- Customer's stated issue
- Relevant order or case reference
- Summary of the problem
- Previous troubleshooting steps
- Reason for escalation

The system should minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

---

## 8. AI Safety Boundary

The AI assistant must not:

- Threaten or intimidate customers.
- Provide legal conclusions.
- Make unauthorized financial decisions.
- Promise outcomes that have not been confirmed.
- Invent company policies.
- Conceal uncertainty.
- Prevent customers from requesting human support.

---

## 9. Human-in-the-Loop Principle

Human support representatives remain responsible for decisions that exceed the AI assistant's approved authority.

The AI assistant is intended to support customer-service operations, not replace human accountability.

---

## 10. Auditability

Escalation decisions should be recorded in an appropriate audit trail.

Where technically possible, the audit record should include:

- Escalation reason
- Detected intent
- Urgency classification
- Relevant knowledge source
- Timestamp
- System outcome

Sensitive customer information should not be unnecessarily included in logs.

---

## 11. AI Governance Requirements

The system should prioritize:

- Customer safety
- Human oversight
- Privacy
- Transparency
- Accurate information
- Controlled AI authority
- Traceable decisions

When there is uncertainty between providing an unsupported answer and escalating the case, the system should favor a safe escalation.
